Will there be transportation?

Yes! We are arranging transportation from a central meeting point near the hotels listed above. Guests will be taken to the church for the ceremony, and then to The House Estate for the reception afterward. Pickup times and the exact location will be provided closer to the date.

We are so grateful that so many of you are traveling such a long way to celebrate with us. We want every one of you there, and making the day as easy as possible, especially for our out-of-town guests, is truly the least we can do.

Any tips for saving on travel?

First, thank you. It means the world to us that you want to celebrate with us, and we truly want everyone there. We know travel is expensive, which is one reason we are arranging transportation for the day.

A few tips that may help:

  • Track flight prices early. On Google Flights, you can turn on price tracking for your travel dates and get an email when fares drop.
  • Compare rates at the recommended hotels above. Booking sites often have good deals on them.
  • Look at bundles. Sites like Expedia often offer savings when you book your flight and hotel together, and a rental car too if you need one.

Prices change often, so it is worth checking back from time to time. We hope these tips help you find a great deal, and we cannot wait to celebrate with you!
